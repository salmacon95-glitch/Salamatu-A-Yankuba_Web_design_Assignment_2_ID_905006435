## <!-- Farmyad  Marketplace-->

Connecting farmers directly with buyers through a simple, modern online marketplace.

Farmyad is a multi-page website that helps local farmers showcase who they are, list their produce, and reach customers without relying on middlemen. Buyers can discover farmers, browse fresh products, and get in touch easily, all from one place.

 <!-- Project Overview -->
 Farmyad is a website designed to bridge the gap between farmers and consumers. Many small-scale farmers struggle to find reliable buyers and fair prices, while many buyers struggle to find fresh, locally grown produce. Farmyad brings both groups together on a single platform.

The website contains five main pages: Home, About, Farmers, Marketplace, and Contact Us. Each page has a clear purpose and is linked through a consistent navigation bar, so visitors can move around the site easily.

<!-- Problem Statement -->

Small and local farmers often face these challenges:

Limited access to buyers beyond their immediate community
Dependence on middlemen who reduce their profits
Little or no online presence to promote their produce
Difficulty in communicating prices and availability to customers

At the same time, buyers often cannot easily find fresh produce directly from the source. Farmyad addresses both sides of this problem.

<!-- Objectives -->
Give farmers a digital space to present themselves and their work
Provide a marketplace where fresh produce can be browsed and discovered
Make it easy for buyers and farmers to contact each other
Build a clean, user-friendly, and responsive website
Demonstrate web development skills learned in class (structure, styling, layout, and interactivity)

<!-- Features -->
 Consistent navigation across all five pages
 Welcoming landing page that explains what Farmyad is at a glance
 Farmer profiles that put a face and story behind the produce
 Marketplace listings showing products with names, images, and prices
 Contact form so visitors can send messages directly
 Responsive layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops
 Nature-inspired design using fresh green and earthy tones

 <!-- Pages of the Website -->

## 1. Home Page

File: index.html

The Home page is the first thing visitors see. It introduces Farmyad and its mission in a quick, engaging way.

What it contains:

A navigation bar linking to all pages
A hero section with a headline, short description, and a call-to-action button (for example, "Visit the Marketplace")
A short overview of what Farmyad offers
Highlights or featured sections that guide visitors toward the Farmers and Marketplace pages
A footer with quick links and basic contact details

Purpose: To make a strong first impression and direct visitors to the most important parts of the site.

## 2. About Page

File: about.html

The About page tells the story behind Farmyad and explains why it was created.

What it contains:

The mission and vision of the platform
The problem Farmyad aims to solve
The values behind the project (fairness, freshness, community, and sustainability)
A short note about the people who built the website

Purpose: To build trust with visitors by explaining who is behind Farmyad and what it stands for.

## 3. Farmers Page

File: farmers.html

The Farmers page introduces the farmers who are part of the Farmyad community.

What it contains:

Profile cards for each farmer, including a photo, name, and location
The types of crops or products each farmer grows or sells
A short description or story about each farmer
A way for visitors to learn more or get in touch with a farmer

Purpose: To humanize the produce by showing the people who grow it, and to help buyers connect with farmers they trust.

## 4. Marketplace Page

File: marketplace.html

The Marketplace is the heart of the website, where visitors can browse the products available.

What it contains:

A grid of product cards, each with an image, product name, price, and the farmer who offers it
Product categories such as vegetables, fruits, grains, and livestock products
Buttons or links for interacting with a product (for example, "Contact Seller" or "Add to Cart")
A clean, easy-to-scan layout so buyers can compare items quickly

Purpose: To let buyers discover fresh produce and see what is available at a glance.

## 5. Contact Us Page

File: contact.html

The Contact Us page gives visitors a simple way to reach the Farmyad team.

What it contains:

A contact form with fields for name, email address, subject, and message
Direct contact information such as email, phone number, and location
A submit button and clear feedback for the visitor after sending a message

Purpose: To make communication easy for buyers, farmers, and anyone with questions or feedback.
