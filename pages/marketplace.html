Products

FarmYad offers a variety of fresh, high-quality agricultural products directly from local farmers across Sierra Leone. Our marketplace features farm produce such as rice, cassava, groundnuts, maize, vegetables, fruits, and other locally grown crops. We connect farmers with buyers, making it easier to sell and purchase fresh produce at fair prices while reducing transportation challenges and the role of unnecessary middlemen. Through FarmYad, we aim to support local farmers, strengthen the agricultural sector, and make fresh farm products more accessible to everyone..
