Background

FarmYad Marketplace was created to close the gap between farmers and the people who need fresh farm produce. Many farmers in Sierra Leone grow plenty of food but struggle to reach buyers at fair prices.

Our platform gives farmers a simple way to list their produce, and gives buyers a trusted place to find, compare and order directly from the source.

Who We are

We are a team of passionate people who believe agriculture can transform lives and communities. FarmYad brings together farmers, buyers and agribusinesses on one digital platform, making it easy to buy or sell farm produce.

By removing the barriers of distance, middlemen and limited market access, we help farmers earn more from their harvest while giving customers fresh, quality food at honest prices.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To connect farmers directly to buyers through a simple, reliable and affordable marketplace, creating fair income opportunities for every farmer.

Our Vision

A thriving agricultural community in Sierra Leone and beyond, where every farmer has access to markets and every family has access to fresh, local food.
